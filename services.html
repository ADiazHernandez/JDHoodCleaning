Services

Everything your commercial kitchen needs to stay fire-safe, code-compliant, and ready for inspection — from routine cleaning to full compliance documentation.

Hood & duct cleaning

We strip the entire exhaust system — hood, ductwork, and rooftop fan — down to bare metal, following NFPA 96 cleaning standards. This is the core service that keeps grease from accumulating into a fire hazard and keeps your system running efficiently.

Fire risk assessment

Every cleaning includes a full assessment of grease buildup, hot spots, and any areas where accumulation could restrict access to fire suppression systems. We flag issues before they become violations or, worse, fires.

Compliance documentation

Every job ends with a dated service tag on the hood and a full photo-documented report — the kind of paper trail your insurer, health inspector, and fire marshal expect to see on file.

Emergency & after-hours service

Failed an inspection or need a same-day cleaning before reopening? Our crews are available 24/7 for urgent jobs, with minimal disruption to your kitchen's operating hours.
