Grease Duct Access Doors

Grease duct access doors provide safe, code-compliant access to commercial kitchen exhaust ducts for routine inspection and cleaning. Proper access helps remove grease buildup, reducing fire hazards, improving system performance, and ensuring compliance with NFPA 96 fire safety standards.

Whether you're installing a new access panel or replacing an existing one, a properly installed grease duct access door makes ongoing maintenance safer, faster, and more efficient.

Hood Hinge Installation

Hood hinge installation allows commercial kitchen exhaust hoods to be safely lifted for easier access during cleaning, inspection, and maintenance. Hinges improve accessibility, help technicians clean hard-to-reach areas, and support compliance with NFPA 96 fire safety standards.

Properly installed hood hinges make routine maintenance faster, safer, and more effective while helping extend the life of your exhaust system.

Rooftop Grease Containment System Installation

A rooftop grease containment system helps prevent grease from leaking onto the roof by capturing runoff from commercial kitchen exhaust fans. This protects the roof from damage, reduces slip hazards, helps maintain a cleaner facility, and supports compliance with environmental and safety regulations.

Proper installation helps extend the life of your roof while simplifying maintenance and cleanup.

Filter Replacement

Regular filter replacement keeps your commercial kitchen exhaust system operating safely and efficiently. Clean, properly functioning filters improve airflow, reduce grease buildup in the ductwork, lower the risk of fire, and help maintain compliance with NFPA 96 standards.

Replacing worn or damaged filters also helps extend the life of your exhaust system and improves overall kitchen ventilation.
