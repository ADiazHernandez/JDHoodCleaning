Pricing questions

How is pricing determined?

Cost depends on hood size, total duct footage, roof access, and how much grease buildup is present at the first visit.

How often do I actually need cleaning?

Most local fire codes require cleaning every 3 to 6 months for standard-volume kitchens, and monthly for high-volume or 24-hour operations.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — plans can be adjusted at any time as your kitchen's volume or inspection requirements change.

Do you offer multi-site contracts?

Yes, our High-volume plan is built for restaurant groups managing several locations under one account.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us about your kitchen and we'll recommend a cadence and give you a firm quote.
